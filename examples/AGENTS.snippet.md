## Subagent Steward

For substantial implementation, debugging, research, or review with potentially independent subtasks, use the installed `subagent-steward` skill before deciding whether to delegate. Resolve its SKILL.md from the available skill catalog rather than a machine-specific path. This instruction requests delegation when that skill's conditions are met, not for every task. Preserve the user's chosen primary model and reasoning effort. Select supported child models and efforts explicitly, normally Luna/high or Sol/medium. Choose concurrency from task needs and runtime capacity without a fixed skill-level cap. Skip loading the skill for routine conversation or tiny edits. If acting as a child, complete the assignment without nested delegation unless explicitly requested by the user. If the skill is not installed, continue the task with available capabilities without claiming its policy was loaded.
