## Subagent Steward

For substantial implementation, debugging, research, or review with potentially independent subtasks, use the installed `subagent-steward` skill before deciding whether to delegate. Resolve its SKILL.md from the available skill catalog rather than a machine-specific path. This instruction requests delegation when that skill's conditions are met, not for every task. Preserve the user's chosen primary model and reasoning effort. Prefer supported Luna/high children, adjusting effort to the actual reasoning burden; choose Sol only with a concrete reason why appropriately supported, higher-effort Luna is a poor fit, normally starting Sol at medium. Set model and effort explicitly. Choose concurrency from task needs and runtime capacity without a fixed skill-level cap. Skip loading the skill for routine conversation or tiny edits. If acting as a child, complete the assignment without nested delegation unless explicitly requested by the user. If the skill is not installed, continue the task with available capabilities without claiming its policy was loaded.
