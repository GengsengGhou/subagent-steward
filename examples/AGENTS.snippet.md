## Subagent Steward

For substantial implementation, debugging, research, or review, use the installed `subagent-steward` skill from the catalog. Preserve the user's primary model, effort, choices, scope, and authorization. Capable Luna/Sol workers default to complete execution through integration, checks, related docs, verification records, and artifact references. Respect project-reserved lead work; the lead retains decisions, communication, actual-artifact acceptance, and risk intervention. Reuse a suitable owner for follow-ups and pass relevant changed constraints or responsibilities to older workers. Reuse fully read, still-available relevant rules/evidence; reread for truncation, changes, lost context, required freshness, or higher-priority instructions. Use stage-appropriate native event/completion waits within runtime and user-update rules, without unjustified short probes or busy polling; combine nonurgent feedback and relay meaningful findings or urgent messages. Require concurrent independent parent work only when the live tool requires it, including for nesting. At capacity, reuse or queue without terminating active work or bypassing limits. Select supported Luna/Sol models and efforts explicitly and flexibly, favoring Luna when both fit. Do not routinely duplicate execution; incomplete delivery ordinarily goes back to its owner. No fixed hierarchy or quota applies. Skip delegation for tiny edits or routine conversation; if the skill is absent, continue without claiming it was loaded.
