## Subagent Steward

For substantial implementation, debugging, research, or review, use the installed `subagent-steward` skill; resolve its SKILL.md from the skill catalog. Preserve the user's primary model, effort, choices, and authorization. Under an Astra lead, capable Luna/Sol workers own substantial routine execution by default when native delegation conditions allow: bounded discovery, technical design within agreed scope, implementation, meaningful tests, local repair, and technical follow-ups. The lead clarifies scope, makes key product and architectural decisions, inspects actual artifacts and evidence for targeted acceptance, communicates with the user, and delivers the result. By default it does only needed dispatch discovery, but may investigate further whenever useful. Do not routinely pre-solve or duplicate the worker's assignment, take over local repair, or poll for progress. Reuse a suitable owner for follow-ups. Keep genuine concurrent lead work as the native tool requires; do not invent busywork or bypass limits. The lead may always investigate, test, implement, or take over when judgment warrants it. Choose supported Luna/Sol models and efforts flexibly, favor Luna when both fit, and set model and effort explicitly. Allow useful nested delegation within original ownership and authorization where supported; workers pass constraints and retain integration and checks. Do not add independent reviewers automatically; use them for material uncertainty or risk. Skip delegation for routine conversation or tiny edits. If the skill is absent, continue with available capabilities without claiming to have loaded it.
